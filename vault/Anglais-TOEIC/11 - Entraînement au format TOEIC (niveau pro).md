# Entraînement au format TOEIC (niveau pro)

> [!abstract] L'essentiel en 30 secondes
> - Cette fiche reprend les **types de questions réels** du TOEIC : *phrases à compléter*, *conversations courtes* et *documents avec plusieurs questions*.
> - Les thèmes sont ceux du **monde professionnel et du quotidien** : réunions, mails, voyages, achats.
> - Chaque banque tire des questions **au hasard** : recommence autant de fois que tu veux.
> - Objectif : répondre en **moins d'une minute** par question, sans perdre le sens du texte.

## Le cours

### 1. Les trois formats à connaître

| Format | Ce qu'on fait | Temps conseillé | Astuce |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phrase à compléter** | Choisir le mot ou la forme qui manque | 15 à 20 s | Regarde ce qui est **avant et après** le blanc : nature du mot, temps, préposition |
| **Conversation courte** | Comprendre qui parle, où, pourquoi, et ce qui va se passer | 30 à 40 s | Lis la question d'abord, puis cherche la **réponse à la fin** de l'échange |
| **Document avec plusieurs questions** | Lire un mail, une annonce, un article puis répondre à 3 ou 4 questions | 3 à 5 min par document | Les questions suivent **l'ordre du texte** |

> [!tip] Méthode pour les phrases à compléter
> 1. Quelle est la **nature** du mot qui manque : nom, verbe, adjectif, adverbe, préposition ?
> 2. Quel **temps** ou quelle **construction** : *to* + infinitif, verbe-*ing*, participe passé ?
> 3. Cherche un **mot-signal** : *since*, *for*, *yesterday*, *if*, *by the time*.
> 4. Élimine deux réponses, puis vérifie la dernière en relisant la phrase.

> [!warning] Pièges classiques
> - **Adjectif ou adverbe** : *a careful driver* mais *drives carefully*.
> - **Préposition fixe** : *responsible **for***, *interested **in***, *depend **on***.
> - **Because** (+ sujet + verbe) ou **because of** (+ nom).
> - **Participe passé** après *have* et *be* (*has been sent*).

### 2. Documents à lire puis questions

Lis chaque document, puis réponds au quiz juste en dessous.

> [!example] Document 1 : Email
> **From:** Laura Mills, Office Manager  
> **To:** All staff  
> **Subject:** Office move  
> Dear colleagues, as you know, our company is moving to a bigger building on **Park Street** on **Monday 3 June**. All employees must pack their personal items by Friday 31 May. The IT team will move the computers over the weekend, so please do not forget to log out and back up your files. There will be a free parking space for each employee in the new building. If you have any questions, please contact me before Wednesday.  
> Best regards, Laura

```quiz Document 1
? Why did Laura write this email?
+ To inform staff about a move
- To announce a new employee
- To cancel a meeting
> Le sujet est « Office move » : un déménagement.

? What must employees do before Friday 31 May?
- Contact the IT team
+ Pack their personal belongings
- Buy a parking space
> « Pack their personal items by Friday 31 May ».

? What will the IT team do?
- Pack the personal items
+ Move the computers over the weekend
- Clean the old building
> « The IT team will move the computers over the weekend ».

? What is an advantage of the new building?
+ Free parking for each employee
- Lower rent
- A restaurant
> « Free parking space for each employee ».
```

## Exercices corrigés

> [!question] Exercice 1
> Complète : *Our manager is responsible ____ the budget.*

> [!success]- Corrigé
> **for** : *responsible for*.

> [!question] Exercice 2
> Complète : *The reports ____ already ____ sent to the clients.* (be, send)

> [!success]- Corrigé
> *have been sent* : present perfect passif.

> [!question] Exercice 3
> Dans une conversation : *« I'd love to, but I have a deadline. »* La personne accepte-t-elle ?

> [!success]- Corrigé
> **Non.** *I'd love to, but…* est un refus poli.

### Conversations de bureau et de la vie courante (31 questions)

```quiz Conversations courtes ~10
? A: « Is Ms Brown available? » B: « She's in a meeting until 3. Can I take a message? » What does B offer?
+ To take a message
- To book a room
- To send an email
> Take a message.

? A: « Could you send me the report by tomorrow? » B: « Sure, no problem. » What will B do?
+ Send the report
- Cancel the report
- Write a new report
> No problem = d'accord.

? A: « I'm calling about the job advert. » B: « Please send your CV to our HR department. » What should A send?
+ A CV
- A report
- A photo
> Send your CV.

? A: « Shall we move the meeting to Friday? » B: « Friday's difficult. How about Thursday? » What does B suggest?
+ Thursday
- Friday
- Monday
> How about Thursday.

? A: « The printer is out of paper. » B: « I'll get some from the storeroom. » What will B do?
+ Get paper
- Call a technician
- Buy a printer
> Get some paper.

? A: « Did you reserve a room for the training session? » B: « Yes, room 4, at 10. » Where is the session?
+ Room 4
- Room 10
- Room 14
> Room 4.

? A: « I'm sorry, I'll be late. The train is delayed. » B: « Don't worry, we'll start without you. » B will:
+ start the meeting
- cancel it
- wait
> Start without you.

? A: « What time does the conference start? » B: « Registration is at 9, and the first talk is at 9:30. » When is the first talk?
+ 9:30
- 9:00
- 10:00
> First talk at 9:30.

? A: « Can I pay by credit card? » B: « I'm afraid we only accept cash. » How can A pay?
+ Cash
- Card
- Cheque
> Only cash.

? A: « I'd like to change my flight to Monday. » B: « There will be a fee of 50 euros. » What is the problem?
+ There is a fee
- The flight is full
- A is late
> Fee = frais.

? A: « Our sales went up by 10% this quarter. » B: « That's great news! » What happened?
+ Sales increased
- Sales fell
- Sales stayed the same
> Went up = ont augmenté.

? A: « Who's going to present the results? » B: « Sara will, she knows the figures best. » Who will present?
+ Sara
- A
- B
> Sara will.

? A: « I can't open this file. » B: « Try downloading it again. » What does B advise?
+ Download it again
- Delete it
- Print it
> Try downloading again.

? A: « We're running out of time. » B: « Then let's skip the last item. » B suggests:
+ skipping the last item
- adding items
- stopping the meeting
> Skip = sauter.

? A: « Is this seat taken? » B: « No, go ahead. » B means:
+ You can sit
- You can't sit
- It's reserved
> Go ahead = vas-y.

? A: « Would you like to join us for lunch? » B: « I'd love to, but I have a deadline. » B:
+ can't come
- will come
- is late
> I'd love to, but… = refus poli.

? A: « How long have you worked here? » B: « Since 2018. » Which is true?
+ B started in 2018
- B started yesterday
- B will leave in 2018
> Since 2018 = depuis 2018.

? A: « The shop is closed on Sundays. » B: « So we must go on Saturday. » When will they go?
+ Saturday
- Sunday
- Monday
> Must go on Saturday.

? A: « I'd like a refund. The jacket is too small. » B: « Do you have the receipt? » What does B ask for?
+ The receipt
- A new jacket
- Identity card
> Receipt = reçu.

? A: « Where can I find the HR department? » B: « Take the lift to the third floor. » Where is HR?
+ On the third floor
- In the basement
- On the roof
> Third floor.

? A: « Are you coming to the party? » B: « Only if I finish my work. » B:
+ might not come
- will certainly come
- will never come
> Only if = seulement si.

? A: « The invoice is wrong. » B: « I'll check it and call you back. » B will:
+ check and call back
- ignore
- send it again
> Call back = rappeler.

? A: « Would you like to leave a message? » B: « No, I'll call again later. » B:
+ will call later
- will wait
- will leave a message
> Call again later.

? A: « We need to hire two more employees. » B: « I'll post the job advert today. » B will:

? A: « I've lost my password. » B: « Click on « Forgot password » to reset it. » What should A do?

- Buy a new computer
> Reset = réinitialiser.

? A: « The delivery should arrive on Tuesday. » B: « Will somebody be there to sign for it? » B asks about:
+ someone to receive it

- the colour
> Sign for = signer à la réception.

? A: « Can you give me a lift to the station? » B: « Sorry, my car is at the garage. » B:

> At the garage = en réparation.

? A: « It's freezing in here. » B: « I'll turn up the heating. » B will:
+ make it warmer

- turn off the lights
> Turn up the heating = monter le chauffage.

? A: « I didn't get your email. » B: « I'll forward it again. » B will:
+ send it again
- delete it
- print it
> Forward = faire suivre.

? A: « Could I have a window seat? » B: « Of course. Here is your boarding pass. » Where are they?
+ At an airport
- At a bank
- At a school
> Seat + boarding pass.

? A: « Two coffees, please. » B: « To drink here or to take away? » Where are they?
+ In a café
- In a hospital
- At a station
> Take away.
```
