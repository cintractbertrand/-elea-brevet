# Test blanc complet

> [!abstract] L'essentiel en 30 secondes
> - 40 questions en 4 séries, sans notes, **comme au vrai test**.
> - Fais-le en une fois, **sans chercher la réponse**, puis lis chaque explication.
> - Recopie tes erreurs dans un carnet et refais le test une semaine plus tard.
> - Pour le listening, refais les scripts de la fiche 09 avec quelqu'un.

## Le cours

> [!tip] Barème indicatif
> - **34 à 40** : très bien, tu es prête.
> - **26 à 33** : solide, retravaille les erreurs.
> - **18 à 25** : revois les fiches de grammaire (04 à 07).
> - **moins de 18** : reprends tout depuis la fiche 00, par petites séances.

## Quiz

```quiz Série 1, grammaire (10 questions)
? My sister ___ in a bank.
- work
+ works
- working
> 3e personne du singulier : -s.

? Yesterday we ___ to the cinema.
- go
+ went
- gone
> Passé de go : went.

? They ___ dinner when I arrived.
- have
+ were having
- are having
> Action en cours au passé, interrompue par « arrived ».

? I haven't seen him ___ Monday.
- for
+ since
- during
> Since + point de départ.

? Look! The children ___ in the garden.
+ are playing
- play
- played
> « Look! » : action en cours.

? If I ___ more money, I would buy a car.
- have
+ had
- will have
> Conditionnel 2 : if + prétérit.

? This exercise is ___ than the last one.
+ easier
- more easy
- easiest
> Easy → easier (-y devient -ier).

? You ___ drive a car at 14. It's illegal.
- don't have to
+ mustn't
- shouldn't
> Interdiction légale : mustn't.

? How ___ brothers do you have?
- much
+ many
- long
> Brothers est dénombrable → many.

? He is the ___ player in the team.
- better
+ best
- most good
> good → better → best.
```

```quiz Série 2, vocabulaire (10 questions)
? You can borrow books from a ___.
- bookshop
+ library
- canteen
> A library prête des livres ; a bookshop les vend.

? The flight is ___. It will leave two hours late.
+ delayed
- cancelled
- booked
> Delayed = en retard.

? Could I have the ___, please? I'd like to pay.
- menu
+ bill
- tip
> The bill = l'addition.

? I'm writing to ___ for the job of waiter.
- hire
+ apply
- rent
> Apply for = postuler.

? She has a sore ___ and a fever.
- foot
+ throat
- hair
> Sore throat = mal de gorge.

? The meeting is ___ to Friday. It's no longer on Wednesday.
+ postponed
- cancelled
- finished
> Postponed = reporté.

? The shop is having a ___ : everything is 30% off.
- sale
- receipt
- ticket
> A sale = des soldes.

? I'd like a single ___ for two nights, please.
- ticket
+ room
- table
> Chambre d'hôtel : single room.

? Please ___ attached the report.
+ find
- found
- finding
> Please find attached = veuillez trouver ci-joint.

? He ___ his keys, so he can't enter the house.
+ lost
- loosed
- looked
> lose – lost – lost.
```

```quiz Série 3, lecture : phrases à compléter (10 questions)
? Mr Brown, our manager, ___ in a meeting at the moment.
+ is
- are
- be
> « Mr Brown » est singulier : is.

? We ___ our homework before dinner every day.
+ do
- does
- doing
> Habitude : we do.

? The restaurant was full, ___ we went to another one.
- because
+ so
- but
> So = donc (conséquence).

? ___ it was raining, they played outside.
+ Although
- Because
- So
> Although = bien que.

? Paul speaks English ___ than his brother.
- good
+ better
- best
> Comparatif de good : better.

? She said she ___ come to the party.
- can
+ could
- must
> Discours rapporté, passé : could.

? I am not sure ___ the answer.
- of
- in
+ about
> « Sure about » = sûr de.

? He is very interested ___ science.
+ in
- on
- at
> Be interested in.

? They live ___ a small flat near the park.
- on
+ in
- at
> Dans un appartement : in.

? I look forward ___ you next week.
- to see
+ to seeing
- seeing
> Look forward to + verbe-ing.
```

```quiz Série 4, lecture de documents (10 questions)
? « Closed on Sundays and bank holidays. » Can you visit on Sunday?
- Yes
+ No
> Closed = fermé.

? « Buy one, get one free! » If you buy 2 T-shirts, you pay for:
+ 1
- 2
- 3
> Buy one, get one free = on en paie un seul.

? « The train leaves from platform 4 at 6:45 pm. » What time?
- 6:15 am
- 4:45 pm
+ 6:45 pm
> Attention : 4 est le quai.

? « Please keep your receipt. You can return items within 14 days. » What should you keep?
+ Your receipt
- Your passport
- Your bag
> Receipt = ticket de caisse.

? « We regret to inform you that your flight is cancelled. » The flight:
- is on time
- is delayed
+ will not happen
> Cancelled = annulé.

? « Dear Ms Taylor, thank you for your application. We would like to invite you for an interview on 5 May. » This email is about:
+ a job
- a holiday
- a bank account
> Application + interview : emploi.

? « Dogs are not allowed in the shop. » You cannot:
- bring a bag
+ bring a dog
- eat
> Not allowed = interdit.

? « Out of order » on a vending machine means:
- Open
- Cheap
+ Not working
> Out of order = en panne.

? « The meeting has been moved from 10 am to 11 am. » The new time is:
- 10 am
+ 11 am
- 9 am
> Moved from… to… : nouvel horaire après « to ».

? « Free Wi-Fi for customers » means:
+ the Wi-Fi costs nothing
- the Wi-Fi is broken
- the customers are free
> Free = gratuit.
```
