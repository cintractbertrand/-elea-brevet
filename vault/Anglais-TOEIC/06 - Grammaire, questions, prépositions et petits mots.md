# Grammaire, questions, prépositions et petits mots

> [!abstract] L'essentiel en 30 secondes
> - **Questions** : mot interrogatif + auxiliaire + sujet (*Where **did** you go?*).
> - **Temps** : *at* + heure, *on* + jour/date, *in* + mois/année/saison. **Lieu** : *at* (point), *in* (dans), *on* (sur).
> - **Connecteurs** : *but, so, because, although, however*.
> - **Relatives** : *who* (personne), *which* (chose), *where* (lieu), *that* (les deux).

## Le cours

### 1. Les mots interrogatifs

| Mot | Sens |
|---|---|
| What | quoi |
| Who | qui |
| Where | où |
| When | quand |
| Why | pourquoi (→ *Because…*) |
| How | comment |
| How much / How many | combien (indén. / dénomb.) |
| How long / How often | combien de temps / à quelle fréquence |
| Whose | à qui |

### 2. Prépositions

| | at | on | in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temps | at 5 pm, at night | on Monday, on 5 May | in July, in 2025, in winter |
| Lieu | at school, at the door | on the table, on the wall | in London, in the box |

Autres : *from … to* (de… à), *for* + durée (*for two hours*), *since* + début, *during* (pendant), *between … and*, *next to*, *opposite* (en face de), *behind*, *in front of*.

> [!warning] Piège
> On dit *on Monday*, mais **in the morning**, **at the weekend** (UK) / **on the weekend** (US), **at night**.

### 3. Pronoms et possessifs

| Sujet | Complément | Possessif (adj.) | Possessif (pron.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | me | my | mine |
| you | you | your | yours |
| he / she / it | him / her / it | his / her / its | his / hers |
| we | us | our | ours |
| they | them | their | theirs |

*It's her book. → It's hers.* Le possessif ne prend **pas d'article** : *my bag*, pas *the my bag*.

### 4. Connecteurs et relatives

| Mot | Sens | Exemple |
|---|---|---|
| but | mais | *I like it, but it's expensive.* |
| so | donc | *It was late, so I left.* |
| because | parce que | *I stayed because I was tired.* |
| although | bien que | *Although it rained, we went out.* |
| however | cependant | *However, we enjoyed it.* |
| who / which / where | relatives | *The man who called…* |

### 5. Articles

- **a / an** pour une chose qu'on mentionne la première fois (*an* devant une voyelle : *an apple*).
- **the** pour une chose connue ou unique (*the sun*).
- **Pas d'article** pour les généralités : *I like music*, *Dogs are friendly*.

## Exercices corrigés

> [!question] Exercice 1
> Pose la question : *She lives in Rome.* (où)

> [!success]- Corrigé
> *Where does she live?*

> [!question] Exercice 2
> Complète : *The film starts ____ 8 pm ____ Friday.*

> [!success]- Corrigé
> *at 8 pm **on** Friday.*

> [!question] Exercice 3
> Relie par une relative : *This is the girl. She won the prize.*

> [!success]- Corrigé
> *This is the girl **who** won the prize.*

## Quiz

```quiz Questions
? ___ do you get up? – At 7 o'clock.
- Where
+ What time
- Who
> « At 7 o'clock » répond à What time.

? ___ is your birthday? – In May.
+ When
- Why
- Which
> In May = quand ?

? ___ people are there in your class? – Twenty-five.
- How much
+ How many
- How long
> People est dénombrable → How many.

? ___ does he play tennis? – Twice a week.
- How long
+ How often
- How much
> Twice a week = fréquence.

? ___ bag is this? – It's mine.
+ Whose
- Who
- Which
> Whose = à qui (possession).

? Why are you late? – ___
- To the bus.
+ Because I missed the bus.
- Yes, I am.
> Why appelle une réponse en Because.

? ___ did you go on holiday? – To Italy.
+ Where
- Who
- When
> « To Italy » répond à une question de lieu : Where.

```

```quiz Prépositions
? I get up ___ 7 o'clock.
+ at
- on
- in
> Heure précise → at.

? My birthday is ___ June.
- on
+ in
- at
> Mois → in.

? The meeting is ___ Monday.
+ on
- in
- at
> Jour → on.

? There's a picture ___ the wall.
+ on
- in
- at
> Posé/accroché sur une surface → on.

? We live ___ London.
- at
+ in
- on
> Ville → in.

? I have studied English ___ four years.
+ for
- since
- during
> Durée → for ; point de départ → since.

? I have studied English ___ 2022.
- for
+ since
- in
> Date de début → since.
```

```quiz Petits mots
? This is not my book. It's ___. (à elle)
- she
+ hers
- her's
> Pronom possessif : hers, sans apostrophe.

? I like my teacher. I talk to ___ every day.
- he
+ him
- his
> Complément → him.

? It was late, ___ we went home.
- but
+ so
- because
> so = donc, introduit la conséquence.

? I was tired ___ I stayed in bed.
- so
+ because
- although
> because = parce que, introduit la cause.

? ___ it was raining, we went to the park.
+ Although
- Because
- So
> Although = bien que (opposition).

? The woman ___ lives next door is a doctor.
- which
+ who
- where
> Personne → who.

? The town ___ I was born is small.
- who
+ where
- which
> Lieu → where.

? She wants ___ umbrella.
+ an
- a
- the a
> Voyelle (umbrella) → an.
```
