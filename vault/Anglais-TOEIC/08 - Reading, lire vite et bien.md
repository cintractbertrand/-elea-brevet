# Reading, lire vite et bien

> [!abstract] L'essentiel en 30 secondes
> - **Lis les questions d'abord**, puis cherche la réponse dans le texte.
> - Repère le **type de document** (mail, annonce, panneau, article) : cela dit déjà beaucoup.
> - Un mot inconnu ? Devine-le avec le **contexte**, ou saute-le : tu n'as pas besoin de tout comprendre.
> - La bonne réponse **reformule** souvent le texte avec d'autres mots.

## Le cours

### 1. Les types de questions

| Question | Ce qu'on te demande | Où chercher |
|---|---|---|
| *What is the purpose of…?* | Le but du texte | Le titre, la première phrase |
| *According to…* | Un détail précis | Le passage qui contient le mot-clé |
| *What is implied?* | Ce qu'on comprend sans que ce soit écrit | Entre les lignes |
| *What does X mean?* | Sens d'un mot dans son contexte | La phrase autour |
| *Which is NOT true?* | Trouver l'intrus | Vérifie chaque réponse |

### 2. Méthode pas à pas

> [!tip] Méthode
> 1. **Titre et type** de document (30 secondes).
> 2. **Questions** : souligne les mots-clés.
> 3. **Lecture rapide** du texte, en cherchant ces mots-clés (ou leurs synonymes).
> 4. **Élimine** les réponses fausses, puis vérifie la bonne dans le texte.

> [!warning] Piège
> *Not*, *only*, *except*, *unless*, *but* : un petit mot peut inverser le sens d'une phrase. Lis-les !

### 3. Documents types

Les documents ci-dessous sont à lire, puis à répondre dans les quiz plus bas.

> [!example] Document A — Sign
> **LIBRARY RULES**  
> Open Monday to Friday, 9 am – 6 pm. Saturday: 10 am – 2 pm. Closed on Sunday.  
> Silence, please. Food and drinks are not allowed.  
> Books can be borrowed for 3 weeks.

> [!example] Document B — Email
> **From:** Emma Clark  
> **To:** All students  
> **Subject:** Trip to the Science Museum  
> Dear students, our trip is on Thursday, 14 March. The bus leaves school at 8:30 am. Please bring a packed lunch and wear comfortable shoes. If it rains, the trip will be moved to Friday. Please give the signed form to your teacher before Tuesday.  
> Best wishes, Emma Clark

> [!example] Document C — Advertisement
> **SUMMER SALE at Fresh Shoes!**  
> 30% off all sports shoes this weekend only. Buy two pairs and get a free bag. Our shop is next to the train station. Open every day until 8 pm.

> [!example] Document D — Text message
> **Lisa:** I'm sorry, I'm late. The train is delayed.  
> **Tom:** No problem. The film starts at 7:30. Where are you?  
> **Lisa:** I'll be there in 15 minutes. Can you buy my ticket?  
> **Tom:** Sure. See you at the cinema!

> [!example] Document E — Article
> **A new way to go to school**  
> In the town of Greenfield, more students now go to school by bike. Last year only 12% rode their bikes. This year, after the town built new cycle paths, the number rose to 35%. The head teacher says it is good for health and for the planet. However, some parents worry about safety when it rains.

> [!example] Document F — Notice
> **LOST:** a black backpack with a blue key ring, lost on bus 12 on Monday evening. It contains a maths book and a calculator. Please call 07 700 900 123. Reward offered.

> [!example] Document G — Job advert
> **Part-time shop assistant wanted.** Saturdays only, 10 am – 4 pm. No experience needed, but you must be friendly and speak English well. Send your CV to jobs@bookshop.example before 30 April.

## Exercices corrigés

> [!question] Exercice 1
> Document A : peut-on boire un café à la bibliothèque ?

> [!success]- Corrigé
> **Non** : *Food and drinks are not allowed.*

> [!question] Exercice 2
> Document B : s'il pleut jeudi, quand a lieu la sortie ?

> [!success]- Corrigé
> **Vendredi** : *If it rains, the trip will be moved to Friday.*

## Quiz

```quiz Document A, panneau
? What time does the library open on Saturday?
- 9 am
+ 10 am
- 6 pm
> « Saturday: 10 am – 2 pm ». 9 am est l'horaire de la semaine.

? Which is NOT allowed?
- Borrowing books
- Reading quietly
+ Eating in the library
> « Food and drinks are not allowed ».

? How long can you keep a book?
- One week
- Two weeks
+ Three weeks
> « Books can be borrowed for 3 weeks ».

? On which day is the library closed?
+ Sunday
- Saturday
- Friday
> « Closed on Sunday ».
```

```quiz Document B, email
? Why is Emma writing?
- To cancel a lesson
+ To give information about a school trip
- To ask for money
> Le sujet est « Trip to the Science Museum » : c'est une information sur la sortie.

? When does the bus leave?
- 8:00 am
+ 8:30 am
- 9:30 am
> « The bus leaves school at 8:30 am ».

? What must students bring?
+ A packed lunch
- A map
- A camera
> « Please bring a packed lunch ».

? What must be given to the teacher before Tuesday?
- Money
- A book
+ A signed form
> « Please give the signed form to your teacher ».

? What happens if it rains on Thursday?
- The trip is cancelled
+ The trip is moved to Friday
- The students stay on the bus
> « Moved to Friday » = reportée, pas annulée.
```

```quiz Documents C et D
? When is the sale?
- All summer
+ This weekend only
- Every Monday
> « 30% off ... this weekend only ».

? What do you get if you buy two pairs?
+ A free bag
- A 50% discount
- A free ticket
> « Buy two pairs and get a free bag ».

? Where is the shop?
- Opposite the school
+ Next to the train station
- Behind the bank
> « Our shop is next to the train station ».

? Why is Lisa late?
- She missed her bus
+ Her train is delayed
- She is sick
> « The train is delayed » (retardé).

? What does Lisa ask Tom to do?
- Wait outside
+ Buy her ticket
- Call her mother
> « Can you buy my ticket? ».
```

```quiz Documents E, F et G
? In Greenfield, the percentage of students going to school by bike was 12% last year. Now it is:
- 12%
- 20%
+ 35%
> « The number rose to 35% ».

? Why did more students start cycling?
- The bus became expensive
+ The town built new cycle paths
- The school is closer
> « After the town built new cycle paths ».

? What worries some parents?
+ Safety when it rains
- The cost of bikes
- Traffic jams in the morning
> « Some parents worry about safety when it rains ».

? Where was the backpack lost?
- At school
- At the cinema
+ On bus 12
> « Lost on bus 12 on Monday evening ».

? What will the finder get?
+ A reward
- A new backpack
- A free bus ticket
> « Reward offered ».

? The job is for:
- Every day
+ Saturdays
- Evenings
> « Saturdays only ».

? What do you NOT need for the job?
- To be friendly
- To speak English well
+ Experience
> « No experience needed ».
```

## Banque d'entraînement

Cette banque contient **37 questions**. À chaque essai, le site en tire **10** au hasard parmi celles que tu n'as pas encore vues, et en change quand tu cliques sur « Recommencer ». Refais-la autant de fois que tu veux.

```quiz Banque : lire des mini-documents ~10
? « Open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm. Closed at weekends. » Can you visit on Saturday?
+ No
- Yes
- Only in the morning
> Closed at weekends = fermé le week-end.

? « Please switch off your phone during the film. » What must you do?
+ Turn off your phone
- Take photos
- Talk quietly
> Switch off = éteindre.

? « Buy one, get one free! » You buy 2 T-shirts. You pay for:
+ one
- two
- three
> Buy one get one free = un acheté, un offert.

? « Wet floor. » What does the sign mean?
+ Be careful, the floor is slippery
- The floor is clean
- Do not enter
> Wet floor = sol mouillé.

? « Out of order. » The machine is:
+ not working
- very cheap
- free
> Out of order = en panne.

? « Staff only. » Who can enter?
+ Employees
- Customers
- Children
> Staff only = réservé au personnel.

? « No entry. » You:
+ cannot go in
- can go in
- must pay
> No entry = entrée interdite.

? « Keep off the grass. » You must:
+ stay off the grass
- cut the grass
- sit on the grass
> Keep off = ne pas marcher sur.

? « Sale: up to 70% off! Ends Sunday. » The sale:
+ finishes on Sunday
- starts on Sunday
- is every Sunday
> Ends Sunday = se termine dimanche.

? « The museum is closed on Mondays. » Today is Monday. You:
+ cannot visit
- can visit
- must buy a ticket
> Closed = fermé.

? « Dear Anna, thanks for your invitation, but I can't come to your party because I'm ill. Love, Ben » Why can't Ben come?
+ He is ill
- He is busy
- He lives far away
> Because I'm ill.

? « Hi Tom, the meeting is moved to Thursday at 2 pm. Please tell Sara. » What must Tom do?
+ Tell Sara
- Cancel the meeting
- Call Thursday
> Please tell Sara = préviens Sara.

? « Hi Jo, I'm at the station. My train is delayed by 30 minutes. » Jo's friend is:
+ late
- early
- at home
> Delayed = retard.

? « We are sorry to inform you that flight BA12 is cancelled. » The flight:
+ will not leave
- is on time
- is full
> Cancelled = annulé.

? « Your appointment with Dr Lee is on 3 May at 10:15. » The appointment is at:
+ 10:15
- 3:15
- 10:50
> 10:15 = ten fifteen.

? « Dear customer, your order has been sent. You will receive it in 3 to 5 days. » When will the order arrive?
+ In 3 to 5 days
- Tomorrow
- In 2 weeks
> In 3 to 5 days.

? « Thank you for your application. We would like to invite you to an interview next Tuesday. » The email is about:
+ a job
- a holiday
- a hotel
> Application + interview = emploi.

? « Sam is learning to play the guitar. He practises every evening for half an hour. » How long does Sam practise?
+ 30 minutes
- 1 hour
- 15 minutes
> Half an hour = 30 min.

? « Lucy has two brothers and one sister. Her youngest brother is five. » How many brothers does Lucy have?
+ Two
- One
- Three
> Two brothers.

? « The Smiths went to Spain last summer. They stayed in a small hotel near the beach. » Where did they stay?
+ In a hotel near the beach
- In a tent
- With friends
> Small hotel near the beach.

? « Mia usually has cereal for breakfast, but today she has toast because there is no milk. » Why toast today?
+ There is no milk
- She doesn't like cereal
- She is late
> Because there is no milk.

? « It's raining, so we are going to stay at home and play games. » What will they do?
+ Play games at home
- Go to the beach
- Watch a match outside
> Stay at home and play games.

? « Jack is taller than his brother, but his sister is the tallest. » Who is the tallest?
+ His sister
- Jack
- His brother
> The tallest = sa sœur.

? « The shop opens at nine, but on Sundays it opens at eleven. » On Sunday the shop opens at:
+ eleven
- nine
- ten
> Sundays = eleven.

? « You must show your ticket at the entrance. Children under 5 enter for free. » Who doesn't pay?
+ Children under 5
- Teachers
- Everybody
> Under 5 = free.

? « The bus to the airport leaves every 20 minutes from platform 3. » How often does the bus leave?
+ Every 20 minutes
- Every hour
- Every 30 minutes
> Every 20 minutes.

? « If you don't have a ticket, you can buy one on the bus. » You can:
+ buy a ticket on the bus
- not take the bus
- get a free ticket
> Buy on the bus.

? « The match was cancelled because of the snow. » Why was the match cancelled?
+ Because of the snow
- Because of a strike
- Because of the heat
> Because of the snow.

? « Emma wants to be a doctor. She studies hard and wants to go to university. » What is Emma's future job?
+ Doctor
- Teacher
- Nurse
> A doctor.

? « Free Wi-Fi for customers. Ask for the password at the counter. » How do you get the password?
+ Ask at the counter
- Buy a coffee
- It's on your phone
> Ask for the password at the counter.

? « Because of repairs, the library will be closed from 10 to 14 June. » When is the library closed?
+ 10 to 14 June
- 10 to 14 July
- All June
> 10 to 14 June.

? « Max forgot his umbrella and got very wet. » What happened to Max?
+ He got wet
- He lost his phone
- He was late
> Got very wet.

? « I'd like a table for two at 8 pm, please. » Where is this person?
+ Probably on the phone with a restaurant
- At a hospital
- At a gym
> Table for two = restaurant.

? « Could I have a single room for two nights? » Who might say this?
+ A hotel guest
- A teacher
- A doctor
> Single room = hôtel.

? « I'd like to return these shoes. They are too small. » Where might this be said?
+ In a shop
- At a station
- At a bank
> Return = rapporter au magasin.

? « How much is this jacket? » This person wants to know:
+ the price
- the size
- the colour
> How much = prix.

? « Do you have this in a larger size? » What does the person want?
+ A bigger size
- A cheaper price
- A different colour
> Larger size = taille plus grande.
```
