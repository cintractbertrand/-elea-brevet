# Grammaire, modaux, comparatifs et conditionnel

> [!abstract] L'essentiel en 30 secondes
> - Après un **modal** (*can, must, should, may, will, would*) : **infinitif sans to**, jamais de -s (*She can swim.*).
> - **Comparatif** : *taller than*, *more interesting than*. **Superlatif** : *the tallest*, *the most interesting*.
> - **Conditionnel 1** : *If it rains, we will stay.* **Conditionnel 2** : *If I had money, I would buy it.*
> - *Much* (indénombrable), *many* (dénombrable), *some* (affirmatif), *any* (négatif et question).

## Le cours

### 1. Les modaux

| Modal | Sens | Exemple |
|---|---|---|
| can / can't | capacité, permission | *I can swim.* |
| could | capacité passée, demande polie | *Could you help me?* |
| must / mustn't | obligation / interdiction | *You mustn't smoke here.* |
| have to | obligation (extérieure) | *I have to wear a uniform.* |
| don't have to | pas obligé | *You don't have to come.* |
| should | conseil | *You should sleep more.* |
| may / might | possibilité | *It might rain.* |

> [!warning] Piège
> *mustn't* = **interdit**, mais *don't have to* = **pas nécessaire**. Ce n'est pas du tout la même chose !

### 2. Comparatif et superlatif

| Adjectif | Comparatif | Superlatif |
|---|---|---|
| court : tall | taller than | the tallest |
| finit par -y : easy | easier than | the easiest |
| long : interesting | more interesting than | the most interesting |
| irrégulier : good | better than | the best |
| irrégulier : bad | worse than | the worst |

Égalité : *as tall as*. Infériorité : *less expensive than* ; *not as expensive as*.

### 3. Conditionnel

| Type | Forme | Exemple |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (réel, possible) | If + présent, **will** + infinitif | *If you study, you will pass.* |
| 2 (irréel, imaginaire) | If + **prétérit**, **would** + infinitif | *If I won, I would travel.* |

> [!tip] Astuce
> Jamais *will* ni *would* juste après *if* : on dit *If it **rains***, pas *If it will rain*.

### 4. Quantité

| Mot | Emploi | Exemple |
|---|---|---|
| some | affirmatif | *I have some friends.* |
| any | négatif, question | *I don't have any money. Do you have any?* |
| much | indénombrable, nég./question | *There isn't much water.* |
| many | dénombrable | *How many books?* |
| a lot of | partout | *a lot of time* |
| a little / a few | un peu de (indén. / dénomb.) | *a little milk, a few apples* |

## Exercices corrigés

> [!question] Exercice 1
> Compare : *This book is (cheap) than that one.*

> [!success]- Corrigé
> *cheaper than* (mot court + -er).

> [!question] Exercice 2
> Conditionnel : *If it (rain) tomorrow, we (stay) at home.*

> [!success]- Corrigé
> *If it **rains** tomorrow, we **will stay** at home.* Type 1.

> [!question] Exercice 3
> *You ____ wear a helmet. It's the law.* (must / should)

> [!success]- Corrigé
> **must** : c'est une obligation légale.

## Quiz

```quiz Modaux
? She ___ speak three languages.
- cans
+ can
- is can
> Après can : pas de -s, c'est un modal.

? You ___ park here. It's forbidden.
- don't have to
+ mustn't
- shouldn't
> mustn't = interdiction forte ; don't have to = pas obligé.

? You ___ come if you don't want to. It's optional.
+ don't have to
- mustn't
- can't
> Optionnel = don't have to.

? You look tired. You ___ go to bed early.
- must
+ should
- can
> Conseil → should.

? ___ you open the window, please?
+ Could
- Must
- Do
> Demande polie → could (ou can).

? He ___ to wear a uniform at school.
- have
+ has
- must has
> have to s'accorde : he has to.

? It's cloudy. It ___ rain later.
- must to
+ might
- mights
> Possibilité → might + infinitif.
```

```quiz Comparer
? My brother is ___ than me.
- more tall
+ taller
- tallest
> Adjectif court + -er.

? This film is ___ than the first one.
- interestinger
+ more interesting
- most interesting
> Adjectif long → more + adjectif.

? She is ___ student in the class.
- cleverer
+ the cleverest
- more clever
> Superlatif de 3+ → the + -est.

? This is ___ day of my life!
+ the best
- the goodest
- better
> good → better → the best.

? Today the weather is ___ than yesterday.
+ worse
- more bad
- badder
> bad → worse → the worst.

? Paul is as ___ as his sister.
+ tall
- taller
- tallest
> as + adjectif de base + as.
```

```quiz Conditionnel
? If it ___ tomorrow, we will stay at home.
- will rain
+ rains
- rained
> Après if, présent (pas de will).

? If I ___ rich, I would buy a boat.
- am
+ were
- will be
> Type 2 : if + prétérit (were s'emploie pour tous).

? If you ___ hard, you will pass the exam.
- would work
+ work
- worked
> Type 1 : if + présent.

? If she ___ more time, she would learn Italian.
+ had
- has
- would have
> Type 2 : if + prétérit.

? I ___ you if I knew the answer.
- will tell
+ would tell
- told
> Type 2 : résultat au conditionnel (would).
```

```quiz Quantité
? I don't have ___ money.
- some
+ any
- many
> Négatif + indénombrable : any.

? How ___ students are there?
+ many
- much
- some
> Students est dénombrable → many.

? There is ___ milk in the fridge.
- a few
+ a little
- many
> Milk est indénombrable → a little.

? Would you like ___ tea?
+ some
- any
- much
> Offre polie → some, même dans une question.

? We have ___ friends here.
- a little
+ a few
- much
> Friends est dénombrable → a few.
```
