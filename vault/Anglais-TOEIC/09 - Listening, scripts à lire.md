# Listening, scripts à lire

> [!abstract] L'essentiel en 30 secondes
> - Le site **n'a pas d'audio**. Solution : **quelqu'un lit le script à voix haute** (une seule fois, à vitesse normale), pendant que tu écoutes sans regarder le texte, puis tu réponds au quiz.
> - Pour progresser seule : lis le script à voix haute, **puis écoute une vidéo ou un podcast en anglais** chaque jour.
> - Avant d'écouter, **lis les questions** : tu sais quoi chercher.
> - Écoute les **mots forts** (noms, chiffres, lieux, verbes) ; les petits mots passent vite.

## Le cours

### 1. Comment utiliser les scripts

1. Ouvre **uniquement les questions** (le quiz).
2. Une personne lit le script (déplié ci-dessous) **une fois**, de façon naturelle. Si tu es seule, enregistre-toi avec le téléphone, ou utilise un synthétiseur vocal en anglais.
3. Réponds aux questions, **puis vérifie avec le script** et les explications.
4. Relis le script à voix haute : repère les mots que tu n'avais pas reconnus.

> [!tip] Méthode
> Écoute en notant sur un brouillon : **qui, où, quand, combien**. Les chiffres sont souvent la clé.

> [!warning] Pièges de l'oral
> - *thirteen* /θɜːˈtiːn/ (accent sur la fin) ≠ *thirty* /ˈθɜːti/ (accent au début) ; idem *fourteen / forty*.
> - *can* /kən/ (faible) ≠ *can't* /kɑːnt/ (fort). 
> - Les contractions : *I'll* = I will, *I'd* = I would.
> - *A quarter to five* = 4 h 45.

### 2. Les types d'extraits

| Type | Exemple | Question typique |
|---|---|---|
| **Annonce** | Gare, aéroport, magasin | Quelle info change ? Quand ? |
| **Dialogue court** | Au téléphone, au restaurant | Que va faire la personne ? |
| **Message** | Répondeur | Pourquoi appelle-t-elle ? |
| **Description d'image** | « The man is carrying a box » | Quelle phrase décrit l'image ? |

## Scripts

> [!example]- Script 1 : Station announcement (à faire lire)
> *Attention, please. The train to Manchester, scheduled for 3:15 pm, is delayed by twenty minutes. It will now leave from platform 5 instead of platform 2. We are sorry for the delay.*

> [!example]- Script 2 : At the restaurant (à faire lire)
> **Waiter:** Good evening. Are you ready to order?  
> **Customer:** Yes. I'd like the chicken salad, and a glass of water, please.  
> **Waiter:** I'm sorry, the chicken salad is finished. We have a fish salad.  
> **Customer:** OK, I'll take the fish salad. And can I have the bill later, please? I have to leave at eight.  
> **Waiter:** Of course.

> [!example]- Script 3 : Answering machine (à faire lire)
> *Hello, this is Sam from the Sunshine Gym. I'm calling about your membership. It ends on 30 June. If you want to renew it, please come before the end of the month. You can also pay online on our website. The gym is closed next Monday for repairs. Thank you, goodbye.*

> [!example]- Script 4 : Two friends (à faire lire)
> **Ben:** Are you doing anything this weekend?  
> **Sara:** I'm going to my grandmother's on Saturday. It's her seventieth birthday.  
> **Ben:** Nice! Why don't we go to the cinema on Sunday afternoon?  
> **Sara:** I'd love to, but I have a lot of homework. Maybe we can go after school on Friday?  
> **Ben:** Good idea. Let's meet at five.

> [!example]- Script 5 : School announcement (à faire lire)
> *Good morning, everyone. Please remember that the school photo is tomorrow at ten o'clock. Students in Year 9 should wear the school uniform. The canteen will be closed at noon because of a meeting, so please bring a packed lunch. Thank you.*

> [!example]- Script 6 : Phone call (à faire lire)
> **Receptionist:** Dr Green's surgery, how can I help you?  
> **Patient:** Hello, I'd like an appointment, please. I have a sore throat and a fever.  
> **Receptionist:** Can you come today at 4:30?  
> **Patient:** I'm at school until five. Is tomorrow morning possible?  
> **Receptionist:** Yes, at 9:15.  
> **Patient:** Perfect, thank you.

## Exercices corrigés

> [!question] Exercice 1
> Tu entends *« The meeting is at fifteen hundred. »* Quelle heure ?

> [!success]- Corrigé
> **15 h** (*fifteen hundred hours* : 3 pm).

> [!question] Exercice 2
> Tu entends *« I'd like a table for two, please. »* Où est-on ?

> [!success]- Corrigé
> **Au restaurant** : *a table for two*.

## Quiz

```quiz Script 1, la gare
? What is the problem?
- The train is cancelled
+ The train is delayed
- The station is closed
> « Delayed by twenty minutes ».

? How long is the delay?
- Two minutes
+ Twenty minutes
- Fifteen minutes
> 3:15 est l'heure initiale. Attention au piège entre 15 et 20.

? What changes about the train?
+ The platform
- The destination
- The price
> « From platform 5 instead of platform 2 ».
```

```quiz Script 2, au restaurant
? What does the customer want first?
+ A chicken salad
- A fish salad
- A pizza
> Il commande d'abord le chicken salad.

? Why can't he have it?
- It is too expensive
+ It is finished
- He is vegetarian
> « The chicken salad is finished ».

? What time does the customer leave?
- Six
- Seven
+ Eight
> « I have to leave at eight ».
```

```quiz Script 3, le répondeur
? Why is Sam calling?
+ About the end of a membership
- To sell a bike
- To invite to a party
> « I'm calling about your membership ».

? When does the membership end?
- 30 July
+ 30 June
- 13 June
> Attention thirty/thirteen : « 30 June ».

? What happens next Monday?
- A party
+ The gym is closed
- A free lesson
> « Closed next Monday for repairs ».
```

```quiz Script 4, deux amis
? What is Sara doing on Saturday?
+ Visiting her grandmother
- Going to the cinema
- Doing homework
> Elle va chez sa grand-mère pour son anniversaire.

? Why can't she go on Sunday?
- She is sick
+ She has a lot of homework
- She is travelling
> « I have a lot of homework ».

? When do they decide to meet?
- Saturday at five
- Sunday at five
+ Friday at five
> « After school on Friday » + « Let's meet at five ».
```

```quiz Script 5, l'école
? When is the school photo?
- Today
+ Tomorrow
- Next week
> « The school photo is tomorrow at ten o'clock ».

? Who should wear the uniform?
- All students
+ Year 9 students
- Teachers
> « Students in Year 9 ».

? Why should students bring a packed lunch?
+ The canteen is closed at noon
- The canteen is dirty
- They go on a trip
> « Closed at noon because of a meeting ».
```

```quiz Script 6, chez le médecin
? What is the patient's problem?
- A broken arm
+ A sore throat and a fever
- A headache
> « I have a sore throat and a fever ».

? Why can't the patient come at 4:30?
+ The patient is at school
- The patient is travelling
- The doctor is busy
> « I'm at school until five ».

? When is the appointment?
- Today at 4:30
+ Tomorrow at 9:15
- Tomorrow at 5:00
> « Yes, at 9:15 » (tomorrow morning).
```

## Banque d'entraînement

Cette banque contient **31 questions**. À chaque essai, le site en tire **10** au hasard parmi celles que tu n'as pas encore vues, et en change quand tu cliques sur « Recommencer ». Refais-la autant de fois que tu veux.

```quiz Banque : mini-dialogues à comprendre ~10
? A: « Excuse me, which platform for the train to Leeds? » B: « Platform 4. » Where are they?
+ At a station
- At a school
- At a cinema
> Platform + train = gare.

? A: « What would you like to drink? » B: « Orange juice, please. » Where are they?
+ In a café or restaurant
- In a library
- In a bank
> Ordering a drink.

? A: « Could you help me find a book on dinosaurs? » B: « Of course, it's on the second floor. » Where are they?
+ In a library or bookshop
- In a hospital
- At a station
> Book + floor.

? A: « Have you got a fever? » B: « Yes, and a sore throat. » Who is A probably?
+ A doctor
- A teacher
- A taxi driver
> Medical questions.

? A: « Tickets, please. » B: « Here you are. » A is probably:
+ an inspector
- a waiter
- a doctor
> Ticket check.

? A: « I'd like to open an account. » Where is A?
+ In a bank
- In a shop
- In a school
> Open an account = ouvrir un compte.

? A: « What's your name and how do you spell it? » B: « Anna Smith, S-M-I-T-H. » What is spelled?
+ The surname
- The street
- The town
> Smith.

? A: « Are you free on Saturday? » B: « Sorry, I'm playing football. » Can B come?
+ No
- Yes
- Maybe tomorrow
> Sorry + playing football = non.

? A: « What time shall we meet? » B: « Half past six at the cinema. » When do they meet?
+ 6:30
- 6:15
- 5:30
> Half past six = 6:30.

? A: « Let's go to the beach. » B: « It's too cold. Why don't we go to the cinema? » What does B suggest?
+ Going to the cinema
- Going to the beach
- Staying at home
> Why don't we… = proposition.

? A: « I missed the bus. » B: « Take a taxi then. » What does B advise?
+ Take a taxi
- Walk
- Wait
> Take a taxi.

? A: « How much is the ticket? » B: « Ten pounds for adults and five for children. » How much for a child?
+ £5
- £10
- £15
> Five for children.

? A: « The film starts at 7:45. » B: « Then let's meet at 7:15. » When do they meet?
+ 7:15
- 7:45
- 8:15
> Let's meet at 7:15.

? A: « Did you do your homework? » B: « Not yet. » B's homework is:
+ not done
- done
- lost
> Not yet = pas encore.

? A: « I can't find my keys! » B: « Look in your bag. » What does B advise?
+ Look in the bag
- Call the police
- Buy new keys
> Look in your bag.

? A: « I'm sorry I'm late. » B: « Don't worry, we've just started. » B is:
+ not angry
- very angry
- surprised
> Don't worry = pas de souci.

? A: « Would you like some more tea? » B: « No, thanks, I'm full. » B:
+ doesn't want more
- wants more
- wants coffee
> No thanks.

? A: « This is my brother, Paul. » B: « Nice to meet you. » What is the situation?
+ An introduction
- A farewell
- An argument
> Nice to meet you = présentation.

? A: « Can I try this on? » B: « The fitting rooms are over there. » Where are they?
+ In a clothes shop
- In a school
- In a hospital
> Fitting rooms = cabines d'essayage.

? A: « Could you speak more slowly, please? » A:
+ doesn't understand well
- speaks too slowly
- is angry
> More slowly = plus lentement.

? A: « I have a reservation for two nights. » Where is A?
+ At a hotel
- At a restaurant
- At a station
> Reservation + nights = hôtel.

? A: « Sorry, the flight is overbooked. » A is probably:
+ an airline employee
- a doctor
- a teacher
> Flight = compagnie aérienne.

? A: « It's raining again. » B: « I know. I hate this weather! » B feels:
+ annoyed
- happy
- surprised
> Hate = déteste.

? A: « Have you ever been to London? » B: « Yes, twice. » B has been to London:
+ two times
- once
- never
> Twice = deux fois.

? « The train arrives at fifteen hundred hours. » What time is it?
+ 3 pm
- 5 pm
- 1 pm
> Fifteen hundred = 15 h.

? « The concert starts at eight o'clock sharp. » What does « sharp » mean?
+ Exactly
- Almost
- Early
> Sharp = pile.

? « I'll pick you up at the station. » The speaker will:
+ collect you
- take a photo
- buy a ticket
> Pick up = venir chercher.

? « Hold on, please. I'll put you through. » Where might this be?
+ On the phone
- In a lift
- At school
> Put through = passer la communication.

? « Could you tell me the way to the post office? » The speaker wants:
+ directions
- stamps
- a map
> The way = le chemin.

? « Take the second turning on the left. » What should you do?
+ Turn left at the second street
- Go straight
- Turn right
> Second turning on the left.

? « It's next to the bank, opposite the library. » The building is:
+ near the bank and the library
- far from town
- in the park
> Next to + opposite.
```
